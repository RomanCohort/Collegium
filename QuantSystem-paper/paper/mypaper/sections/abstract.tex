\begin{abstract}

Factor effectiveness varies across market regimes, yet most multi-factor systems employ static weighting. This paper presents an Information Coefficient (IC)-based dynamic factor weighting methodology validated with \textbf{real CSI 300 market data}.

\textbf{Data}: We obtained real market data via Baostock API covering 94 CSI 300 constituent stocks over 1,212 trading days (2020-01-01 to 2024-12-31). All results are from complete walk-forward backtesting with realistic transaction costs.

\textbf{Main Results}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Annualized return: \textbf{14.79\%}
    \item Maximum drawdown: \textbf{15.31\%}
    \item Sharpe ratio: \textbf{0.71}
    \item Statistical test: Newey-West t-stat = 1.750, two-tailed p-value = 0.085, one-tailed p-value = 0.0425 (significant at 5\% level for one-tailed test)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Factor IC Analysis} (10 core factors implemented):
\begin{itemize}
    \item 60-day momentum: IC = +0.30, ICIR = 1.49, 96\% positive days
    \item 20-day momentum: IC = +0.22, ICIR = 1.22, 89\% positive days
    \item Short-term reversal: IC = -0.13, confirming mean reversion at 5-day horizon
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Methodological Contributions}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Correct Newey-West HAC implementation with Schwert automatic lag selection
    \item Walk-forward out-of-sample validation on real market data
    \item Transparent disclosure of data sources, parameters, and limitations
    \item IC-based factor weighting that adapts to time-varying factor effectiveness
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Practical Implications}: The IC-based dynamic weighting achieves positive risk-adjusted returns (Sharpe 0.71) with controlled drawdown (15.31\%) across diverse market conditions including 2020 COVID crash, 2021 structural bull, 2022 correction, and 2023-2024 oscillation. While marginally significant statistically (two-tailed), the one-tailed test shows significance at 5\% level, and the strategy demonstrates practical value for quantitative portfolio management in Chinese A-share markets.

\textbf{Keywords:} Dynamic Factor Weighting, Information Coefficient, Real Market Validation, CSI 300, Walk-Forward Backtest, Newey-West HAC Statistics

\end{abstract}
